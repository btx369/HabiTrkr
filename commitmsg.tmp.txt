Fix opening a note while a search is active, and make titles writable

Three defects, all in the notes list and viewer.

**A filtered note could not be tapped.** The header closed the search on `mousedown`
whenever the press landed outside the search field. That cleared the query, so the list
was restored with every note *between* mousedown and mouseup: the card the user aimed
at was replaced by a different one before the click completed, and the click landed on
an element with no handler. The only way to open a filtered result was to dismiss the
search first.

Collapsing the field and clearing the query are now separate. The field still hides at
once, but the clear waits one turn, so the list holds still for the whole press. Escape
and the close button still clear immediately, since no press needs protecting there.

**A title could not be typed.** The title field saved on every keystroke. Each save
called up into the page, which replaced `noteToView`, which re-rendered the viewer and
dropped it out of title-editing mode: the field vanished after the first letter and
everything typed afterwards went nowhere. It now commits on Enter or on losing focus,
which is the whole interaction, so nothing is lost by waiting.

**A note was judged empty on its body alone.** The title was not consulted, so a note
given only a title was never created, and an existing one was deleted as soon as its
body was cleared. A title is writing the note, so `isNoteEmpty` now treats one as
content. This is what made the title route appear to do nothing.

Also removes 22 leftover `console.log` calls, including several on the render path and
a group in the audio helpers that logged part of the note body on every call.

Two new behavioural suites, both driving the real app in a browser:
- scripts/verify-note-open-with-search.mjs presses, inspects the DOM mid-press, and
  asserts the card under the cursor is still the one aimed at.
- scripts/verify-note-title.mjs types a title, commits with Enter and with a blur, and
  creates a note that has nothing but a title.

One note on method: `Input.insertText` is used for typing. Dispatching a keyDown
carrying `text` *and* a `char` event inserts the character twice, which made an earlier
probe report a doubling bug the app did not have. That is worth knowing before trusting
a failing typing test here.
